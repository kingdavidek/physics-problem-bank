Zorp and you

What do you think of Zorp?

Zorp is the little alien in our practice app. You have just seen him talk, cheer and jump. Now we would love your ideas.

This is just for fun. It is not a test. Nothing is marked. There are no right answers.

Pick one or two prompts. Write or draw one idea on a sticky note. Do not put your name on it. Skip any prompt you like.

PROMPT 1

What did you think when Zorp cheered?

Any answer is fine: a word, a sentence or a doodle.
PROMPT 2

Was there a moment Zorp got in the way? Tell us about it.

It is useful to hear this. Honest is best.
PROMPT 3

When Zorp talked, what did you look at?

Tell us what you noticed.
PROMPT 4

If Zorp could say one sentence when a question goes wrong, what would be kind and helpful?

Write the sentence as he would say it.
PROMPT 5

If Zorp could do one new thing, what would it be?

A new move, a new job, anything.
PROMPT 6

If Zorp made a sound in our classroom, when would that be fun, and when would it be too much?

Think about how the room would feel.
No names. Not even initials.
No marks. Nobody is judging you.
Notes are thrown away once your teacher has read them.

Your notes are not typed into any app and are not kept. Thank you for helping.

Teacher sheet (keep this one)

Zorp evaluation: 15 to 20 minutes

Teacher-led, offline, anonymous. Nothing is typed into the app. Full plan: docs/ZORP_EVALUATION.md. Results: blank until a session has been run.

Session

MinWhat
0 to 2Frame it: fun, not a test, no names, skip anything.
2 to 9Showreel, 6 steps (below).
9 to 10Quiet moment to think.

16 to 18Notes folded into a pile or box, face down; no one watching who wrote which.
18 to 20Optional chat: read two or three notes aloud.

S1 and S2 (under 13): projector only, no pupil accounts. Older classes may try their own accounts, optional, sounds left off.

Showreel: /styleguide#zorp-charm

First press Preview at full motion. Zoom in (Ctrl and +) for the back row. Volume low. Warn before sound.

  1. Origin line, talking
  2. Reward: soft fist-pump
  3. Same reward, soft body off (A/B)
  4. Corner bubble, talking
  5. Sound on (quiet preview cues)
  6. Sound off (silent; the default)

Observe while it runs (tick any you see)

  • Eyes go to Zorp, then back to the lesson or question within a few seconds
  • Own accounts only: time to press Continue (roughly how many seconds)
  • Smiles and laughter
  • Irritation or eye-rolling on the 2nd or 3rd repeat
  • Anyone asks to turn it off or skip
  • Sound acceptable in the room (no covered ears, no noise wave after)
  • Reading lines aloud, or waiting for the mouth before reading
  • Confusion about bot versus person
  • Upset at the "home" line in the origin story
  • Distraction when you go back to the work (still chatting a minute later)

Reduced motion: if anyone uses it, check quietly that the still version worked for them.

Success looks like

  • E is at least two thirds of E plus D (at least 10 notes with a lean, 2 classes)
  • No unresolved C note, no upset flag
  • Sound judged fine only when opt-in; otherwise it stays off by default

Tune constants only (talk caps, flap rates, soft body share, sound gain). The list and ranges are in ZORP_EVALUATION.md section 10. The voice-blip option (D7, hidden "chatter") stays hidden unless all section 10.4 conditions hold.

Privacy and safeguarding

  • No names, no photos (not of faces, not of notes), no recording
  • Notes destroyed the same day, once the table is filled in. Keep counts and paraphrased themes only
  • Everything optional; stop the sound whenever anyone asks
  • Anything that worries you about a pupil: your school's safeguarding route, not this sheet
