5 Mass and weight

Mass is amount of matter in kilograms. Weight is the gravitational force on that mass, in newtons. Mass stays the same wherever you take the object. Weight is a force and can change if gravity changes. On the Moon the mass of a shot-put stays the same; its weight is smaller.

Worked example: Near Earth, a \(1\,\mathrm{kg}\) mass has a weight of about \(10\,\mathrm{N}\). On the Moon the same 1 kg still has mass 1 kg, but a smaller weight.
