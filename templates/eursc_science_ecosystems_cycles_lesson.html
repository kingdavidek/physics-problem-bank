The water cycle names public stages water can move through. It is not a private diary. Public stages water can move through: evaporate, condense, then precipitate (rain in this schematic). The sequence repeats.

Worked example: Water from a class tray evaporates (A), condenses on a cool lid (B), then drips as rain in the model (C). The same water can repeat the loop.

8 Photosynthesis and respiration

Photosynthesis and respiration are word-equation ideas at S3. They are not a demand to log meals here. Photosynthesis stores a food idea using light. Respiration can release energy from food. Both are word equations in this S3 model, not a plate photo.

Worked example: Photosynthesis (word equation): carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (in light). Respiration (word equation): glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water. Carbon dioxide is named in words on first use here, not only as CO2.
