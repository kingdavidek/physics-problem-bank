3 Dichotomous keys

A dichotomous key asks one checkable feature at a time. A first couplet, B second couplet, C named group. A key that splits on one checkable feature at a time (yes or no). Public classroom cards, not a private home collection.

Worked example: Couplet A: “Has wings?” Yes goes toward a named flying insect. No goes to couplet B (“Has a shell?”) and then to named group C (a snail in the class tray). Another pupil could follow the same yes/no steps.
