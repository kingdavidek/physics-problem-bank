3 Gametes

A gamete A sex cell. The female gamete is the egg (ovum). The male gamete is the sperm. A bone cell is not a gamete. A rumour is not a gamete.

Example: An egg is a gamete. Non-example: A muscle cell in the uterus wall is not a gamete.

4 Menstrual cycle

The menstrual cycle is a repeating sequence, not a one-off event. Teaching this must not become a roll-call of who has periods.

  1. A the uterus lining thickens.
  2. B an egg is released (ovulation Release of an egg from an ovary, typically once per cycle if the cycle is typical.).
  3. C if the egg is not fertilised, the lining is shed (a period).

5 Fertilisation

Fertilisation Egg and sperm joining. It usually happens in the oviduct, not in the uterus itself.

Worked sequence: sperm meets egg (usually in the oviduct) → they join → the early embryo can implant in the uterus lining. This is a mechanism, not a request for anyone’s private history.

6 Clinical language in class

Use the official names. Do not sensationalise. Do not collect who has started periods or who has had sexual contact. Personal questions go to a teacher or health professional, not this app.
