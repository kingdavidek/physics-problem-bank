1 Plan 1.2.8 S1

Choose a menu using food-group evidence from 1.2.1–1.2.7. Include more than one group. Use label energy and ingredients for packaged items. Do not ask classmates to describe private eating habits.

Worked example: Fictional Group A plans a bean stew with rice and a side salad. They note protein (beans), carbohydrate (rice) and vegetables, and they record the method so another group could repeat it. They do not collect classmates' private eating lists.
