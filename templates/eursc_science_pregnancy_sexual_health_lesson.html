1 Intercourse 1.4.3 S1

Sexual intercourse can allow sperm to enter the vagina and swim toward an egg. That is the clinical mechanism. It is not a question about anyone in the room.

Worked sequence: intercourse → sperm may meet an egg → fertilisation if they join → pregnancy if the early embryo implants. Each step can fail; none of them is a prompt for personal stories.

8 Getting help

Personal decisions about sex, contraception, pregnancy, identity or infections belong with a trusted adult or qualified health professional. This page teaches curriculum knowledge. It does not store relationship lists, sexual history or test results. S1 Integrated Science is complete at the end of this unit on the topic path.
