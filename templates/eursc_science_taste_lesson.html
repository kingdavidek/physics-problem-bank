2 Taste and smell

Flavour uses taste and smell together. A fictional blocked-nose case can make food seem bland because smell is reduced.

Worked example: Fictional Sam has a blocked nose during a cold. The same soup tastes blander than usual because smell is reduced, even though the five tastes are still there.

3 Colour and context

The colour of a drink can change how it is judged. The same yoghurt dyed different colours can be labelled differently. That is a cue effect, not a private menu upload.
