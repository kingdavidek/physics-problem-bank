7 Kilojoules and kilocalories

This course treats 1 kcal as 4 kJ (a rounded S1 figure). Two servings means twice the energy on the per-serving line.

Worked example: A fictional pack lists 200 kcal per serving. \(200 \times 4 = 800\,\mathrm{kJ}\). The other way: \(840\,\mathrm{kJ} \div 4 = 210\,\mathrm{kcal}\). Use the pack line, not a classmate's plate.
