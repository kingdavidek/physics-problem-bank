1 Infectious vs noninfectious 2.2.3 S2

An infectious disease can pass from a source along a route to a new host. A noninfectious disease does not spread that way. Does not spread along an infection chain. Treating a noninfectious condition as catching can stigmatise a person who is not a source of infection.
