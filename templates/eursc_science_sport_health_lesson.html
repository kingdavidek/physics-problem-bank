3 Antagonistic muscles

A is the muscle above the bone; B is below. They pull in opposite ways: when one shortens, the other typically relaxes. That is an antagonistic pair. Two muscles that pull in opposite ways at a joint. When one shortens, the other typically relaxes.

Worked example: At the elbow, biceps shortens to bend the arm while triceps relaxes; triceps shortens to straighten it while biceps relaxes.
