5 Non-additive volume

50 cm³ of water plus 50 cm³ of ethanol often gives less than 100 cm³. Particles pack into spaces. Volume is not always additive. Mass is still conserved.

Worked example: A group measures 50 cm³ of water and 50 cm³ of ethanol, mixes them, and reads about 96 cm³. Mass still adds. Volume does not have to.
