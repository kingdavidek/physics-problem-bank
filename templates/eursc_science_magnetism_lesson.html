5 Electromagnets

An electromagnet is a current-made magnet that can be switched. A coil that drops paperclips when the current stops is a public demo. A magnet made by current in a coil. Closing the switch turns it on; opening the switch turns it off.

Worked example: A classroom coil is wound on a nail and connected to a low-voltage cell through a switch the teacher supplies. Switch closed: the nail picks up paperclips. Switch open: the clips drop. The electromagnet is on or off with the current; this is not a strength league and not a \(V = IR\) calculation.
