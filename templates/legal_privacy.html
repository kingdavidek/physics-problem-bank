Privacy notice

How Problem Bank uses personal data (UK GDPR).

Shorter version for students →

Who we are

The controller is {{ controller_name }}. Contact: {{ privacy_contact_email }}.

ICO registration: {{ ico_registration_number }}.

This service is aimed at people aged 13 and over in the UK. We do not ask for your real name, school, photos, or payment details.

What we collect
  • Account: email, handle, password hash, last login time
  • Study record: practice attempts, quiz scores, lesson progress, streaks, badges, revision plans, reflections you type
  • Teacher / classes (optional): if you turn on teacher tools, we store that flag, class names, join codes, handle invites, a class activity log, and frozen set-work scores. If you join a class (by code or by accepting an invite), we store that membership. Teachers can download roster and set-work CSVs that use handles, never emails. We do not store a school name unless you type one into a class title.
  • Social: follows, challenges, suggestions, blocks, reports
  • Settings: visibility, avatar (emoji), theme, sound, digest opt-in, and whether you have already seen the Guide intro and section tours
  • Security: hashed rate-limit keys (not raw IP addresses), API token hashes

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. Session and remember-me cookies are needed to keep you signed in.

Why we use it
  • Contract: to run your account and study features
  • Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention, moderation
  • Consent: optional weekly recap email, and lesson AI assist only if you (and the site operator) turn it on
How long we keep it
  • Account and study data: for as long as the account exists, then deleted immediately on request
  • Teacher classes, join codes, handle invites, class activity logs, and frozen set-work: with the teacher’s account. Class memberships, invites you received, and a student’s assignment answers die with that student’s account. Pending invites older than 14 days are removed.
  • Inactive accounts: warning after 24 months without login; deletion after 30 months
  • Rate-limit records: 7 days. Lesson-assist usage counters: 30 days
  • Email send logs: 12 months. Resolved reports: 12 months after resolution
  • Backups: 14 days. Deleted data leaves live systems immediately and backups within 14 days
Your rights

You can access, export, or delete your data from Settings once your email is confirmed. You can also email {{ privacy_contact_email }}.

You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Who else sees data

Hosting and (if configured) email providers process data for us under their contracts. MathJax/Pyodide may load from a CDN (visitor IP). Lesson AI is off in production unless explicitly enabled; if enabled it receives lesson text and your typed question only — never your handle or email.

If you join a class (by code or by accepting a handle invite), the teacher of that class can see your class progress: class averages, quiz summaries, weak topics, skill-gap labels, and set-work completion (how many frozen questions you finished and your score). They cannot see your free-text reflection notes, your email, or your password. Only the teacher can take you off the class — there is no Leave button. Account deletion removes class memberships, invites, and assignment answers.

See Terms and the subprocessors list in the project documentation.
