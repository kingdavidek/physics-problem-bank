1 Water in food and life 1.2.1 S1

Living things and many foods contain a lot of water. In the kitchen it is the usual solvent: salt, sugar and flavours dissolve in it. The liquid that dissolves other substances. Water is the usual solvent in food and in the body.

2 Proteins

Proteins are especially for growth and repair. Sources include beans, fish, eggs and yoghurt. Heat or acid can denature them (an egg white setting). 3 Fats

Fats and oils store a large amount of energy and carry some vitamins. Olive oil is a plant fat; butter is an animal fat. Per gram they are usually more energy-dense than carbohydrate.
