2 Two kinds of charge

There are two kinds of charge. Opposite charges attract; like charges repel. A and B are schematic charges, not a ranking of hair. Opposite charges attract (pull together). Like charges repel (push apart). Plus and minus marks name the two kinds; colour alone is not enough.

Worked example: Two balloons rubbed on a jumper in a teacher demo: unlike charges pull together; two balloons given the same kind of charge push apart. The class names attract or repel. This quiz does not collect shock stories.
