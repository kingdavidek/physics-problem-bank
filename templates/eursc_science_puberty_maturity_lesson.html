1 Physical changes 1.4.1 S1

At puberty many young people grow faster and develop new body hair and other typical changes. These are general patterns. Classwork does not inspect anyone and does not rank who “looks older”.

3 Hormones

Hormones are chemical messengers in the blood. They help trigger puberty changes. Media images of “ideal” teenage bodies are not a measurement of healthy puberty.

4 Variation and maturity

The age when puberty starts varies. Starting earlier or later than a friend can still be healthy. Sexual maturity here means the body becoming able to produce mature gametes — at a time that varies. Sam and Lee in a scenario must not be mocked for timing.

5 Where to get help

Personal questions about body changes belong with a teacher or qualified health professional. This app does not store health stories, timing lists or mood files. Continue with labelled anatomy in the next module if the class is following the unit order.
