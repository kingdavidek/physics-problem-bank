2 Receptor density

Fingertips are modelled as denser in receptors than some other regions, so two points are easier to tell apart. Density is a model, not a ranking of whose skin is toughest.

Worked example: In a teacher-approved two-point test on a fingertip, two pins a few millimetres apart can feel like two points. On a less dense region the same gap can feel like one. Consent first; this is not a class skin ranking.
