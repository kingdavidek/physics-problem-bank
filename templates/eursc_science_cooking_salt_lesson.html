Concentration is amount in a volume. More salt in the same volume is more concentrated. Compare fairly: keep volume the same or calculate per cm³. 5 g in 100 cm³ means 10 g in 200 cm³ of the same brine.

4 Crystallisation

When water evaporates, salt remains as crystals. A white crust on a dried brine pan is crystallised salt. If no more salt will dissolve, the solution is saturated at that temperature.

5 Preservation

Salt draws water out of many microbes, so salted fish can keep longer. It is not complete sterility: clean handling still matters. Cucumber slices go floppy because water leaves the cells toward the salt.
