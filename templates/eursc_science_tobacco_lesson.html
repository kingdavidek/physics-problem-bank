3 Industry influence

A stylish advert is marketing, not independent scientific evidence. Industry influence can push uptake. Branding that looks sporty is still not a health study. Pupils are not asked to name brands they like.

Worked example: A fictional poster shows a smiling athlete next to a nicotine product. The class asks: was this tested in a controlled study, or is it designed to sell? Fame is not evidence.

Vaping is not the S2 “harmless swap”. Important long-term questions remain. Nicotine in many products can still addict. The quiz does not list devices anyone has tried.
