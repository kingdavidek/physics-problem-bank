6 Sport and oxygen demand

In hard exercise, muscles need more oxygen. Pulse often rises. After a sprint, breathing can stay fast while extra carbon dioxide is cleared. Breathing, blood and cell respiration work together.

8 Buoyancy

Buoyancy (upthrust) is the upward force from a liquid. Denser salty water can make floating easier. The online page does not replace a supervised pool session.
