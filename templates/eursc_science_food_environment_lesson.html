4 Food lifecycle

A food lifecycle runs from produce through use to waste. A produce, B use, C waste. Schematic stages, not a private plate survey. A sequence: produce, then use, then waste. Transport and packaging can sit inside those stages in a class diagram.

Worked example: A fictional public case follows wheat from a named farm (produce), through a bakery loaf sold in a shop (use), to leftover crusts in a municipal bin (waste). The class labels the three stages. Nobody photographs a home plate.
