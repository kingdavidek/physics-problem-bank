Privacy, in plain words

For students aged 13+.

What we know about you. Your email, a username you pick, your password (stored as a secret hash), and your study work: questions you try, quiz scores, streaks, and notes you type.

Who can see it. By default, strangers on the internet cannot see your study activity. Only your username is shown unless you choose a public profile. Public means anyone on the internet, including people who are not logged in.

How to delete it. Open Settings → Your data → Delete my account. Or ask a parent to email {{ privacy_contact_email }}.

Don’t type secrets in notes. Do not put your real name, school, address, or phone number in reflections or share notes.

How to complain. Email {{ privacy_contact_email }}. You can also contact the ICO (the UK privacy watchdog) at ico.org.uk.
