Privacy, in plain words

For students aged 13+.

What we know about you. Your email, a username you pick, your password (stored as a secret hash), and your study work: questions you try, quiz scores, streaks, and notes you type. We also remember if you have already seen the intro, so it does not play again on a new device.

Who can see it. By default, strangers on the internet cannot see your study activity. Only your username is shown unless you choose a public profile. Public means anyone on the internet, including people who are not logged in. If you join a teacher’s class (with a code or by accepting an invite), that teacher can see class progress (including skill-gap labels) and whether you finished set work they assigned, but not your private notes or email. Only the teacher can take you off the class — there is no Leave button.

How to delete it. Open Settings → Your data → Delete my account. Or ask a parent to email {{ privacy_contact_email }}.

Don’t type secrets in notes. Do not put your real name, school, address, or phone number in reflections or share notes.

How to complain. Email {{ privacy_contact_email }}. You can also contact the ICO (the UK privacy watchdog) at ico.org.uk.
